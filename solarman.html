★ Two Golden Daily Checks
Build these as habits — everything else is secondary
1
6–7 AM — Sunrise SOC
Should read 20–25%. Below 18% consistently = grid pulled overnight. Your B-corrected target floor is 21%.
2
5–6 PM — Sundown SOC
Clear day → 100%. Cloudy → 55–60%. If a clearly sunny day shows below 90% at 5 PM = heavy load or system issue.

🚩 Red Flags — Act on These
Production = 0 during daylight on clear sky
Inverter not waking up. Restart first, then call contractor if it persists.
Battery SOC stuck at same % all morning
BMS or WiFi comms glitch. Restart app and inverter.
Load shows 0 W at all times
Monitoring sensor issue only — not an electrical problem, but you're flying blind.
Grid import 1–2 kW sustained on a clear day
Panel underperformance or you're overloading at that moment. Check what's running.
Daily PV Gen consistently 15–18 kWh on sunny days
Panel soiling, partial shading, or a string issue. Clean panels and inspect connections.
Sunrise SOC below 18% three days in a row
Overnight loads consistently exceeding battery. Reduce fans/LEDs overnight or plan for +2 panels in year 2.
